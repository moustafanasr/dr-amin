## Brand opportunity

**Positioning:** international neurosurgical experience + accessible, understandable human-centered communication.

**Not positioning:** "number one," guaranteed outcomes, impossible recovery times, invented success statistics, fake testimonials or unverified hospital affiliations.

## Information architecture

Home → Meet the Doctor → Explore Specialties → Watch Media → Contact/Booking.

The deliberate visual approach is restrained premium medical editorial, with real doctor images, bespoke geometric mark, calm ivory surface, navy authority, copper warmth, asymmetric compositions and motion for hierarchy—not a generic blue hospital kit.

## Product decisions

- Page 1 combines a meaningful intro, four areas of interest, trust from credentials rather than statistics, media, and a patient journey.
- Dedicated subpages avoid cramming all content into one homepage.
- Contact is explicitly a draft because locations/phone/booking links are not verified.
- Reduced motion, semantic controls and responsive layout are first-class.
- GitHub Pages preview is completely static; WordPress migration is a later engineering step.
