## Design system

- Concept: editorial, human-crafted clinical portfolio — *precision without coldness*.
- Colors: midnight navy `#122a3b`, paper `#faf8f5`, copper `#bc8369`, stone `#eee8df`.
- Fonts: Manrope (Latin), IBM Plex Sans Arabic, Cormorant Garamond (Latin editorial accent). Local sans fallback for offline mode.
- Key components: two-column split hero with genuine doctor portrait, editorial specialty index, credential timeline, restrained motion, embedded video library and no invented proof badges.
- Animation: GSAP + ScrollTrigger as progressive enhancement; Three.js minimal 3D ornament; reduced-motion support; no dependency on animation libraries for content visibility.
