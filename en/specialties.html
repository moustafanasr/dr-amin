Spine surgery

Assessment of spinal conditions and consideration of suitable surgical or non-surgical options.

Conditions & procedures

  • Herniated disc treatment
  • Endoscopic spine surgery
  • Minimally invasive spine surgery
  • Spinal stenosis and nerve pain

Endoscopic and minimally invasive approaches when appropriate

Interventional nerve pain management

Interventional procedures for selected causes of nerve-related and spinal pain.

Conditions & procedures

  • Radiofrequency ablation
  • Nerve root injections

Techniques & approach

Targeted interventions following assessment and discussion of options
