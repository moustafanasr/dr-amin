Meet Dr. Amin Elghanam

Dr. Amin Elghanam

A Consultant Neurosurgeon and Spine Surgeon whose professional record includes a doctorate in neurosurgery and specialist training at university hospitals in France.

His listed areas of practice include microsurgery, minimally invasive spine surgery, cerebrovascular surgery and interventional approaches to selected types of nerve pain.
